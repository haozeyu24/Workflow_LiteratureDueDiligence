# Topic

Shared host-pathogen mechanisms and host pathway convergence across KSHV, dengue virus, hepatitis C virus, coxsackievirus, HIV, and Ebola virus, with emphasis on virus-human AP-MS/interactome/proteomics evidence and mechanistic host pathway studies.

The desired literature pool should support:

- mapping viral proteins and infection phenotypes to host proteins, complexes, and pathways;
- comparing mechanisms across viruses even when viral proteins do not share exact human protein targets;
- selecting high-priority findings for expert review by host-pathogen mechanism collaborators;
- grounding a future community-of-agents workflow in known literature and datasets.

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers: named viruses plus interaction, interactome, host factor, proteomics/AP-MS, host pathway, or pathway-convergence language.
- Comparator scope: cross-virus host-pathogen mechanism studies are allowed if anchored to named viruses or directly relevant host-pathogen pathway convergence.
- Secondary synthesis context: Krogan AP-MS data and collaborator evaluation should help prioritize after retrieval, not silently exclude papers before abstract review.
- Deferred adjacent biology: clinical, vaccine, diagnostic, epidemiology, and antiviral-screen literature without host mechanism evidence.
