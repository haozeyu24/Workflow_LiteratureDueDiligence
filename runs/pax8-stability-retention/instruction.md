# Instruction

Identify papers relevant to understanding PAX8 protein stability, degradation, localization, nuclear retention, and closely related regulatory mechanisms.

Prioritize papers that directly study PAX8, but also preserve strong comparator papers on other PAX family members, especially PAX2, PAX5, and PAX6, when they illuminate post-translational control, protein turnover, subcellular localization, chromatin retention, or closely related regulatory machinery.
