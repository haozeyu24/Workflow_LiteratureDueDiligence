# Topic

The scientific objective is to understand how PAX8 protein abundance and nuclear behavior are regulated.

Key areas of interest include:

- PAX8 protein stability
- PAX8 degradation pathways
- ubiquitin-proteasome control of PAX8 or close PAX comparators
- SUMOylation, phosphorylation, acetylation, hydroxylation, or other post-translational regulation of PAX factors
- nuclear localization, nuclear import, nuclear retention, nucleocytoplasmic transport, and chromatin-associated mobility
- mechanistic comparator papers on PAX2, PAX5, and PAX6 when they help interpret possible shared regulation across the PAX family
