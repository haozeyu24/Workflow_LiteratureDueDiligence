# Request

Run a fresh literature-screening and full-text-review workflow to identify papers relevant to a review article on protein-level regulation of PAX transcription factors, especially PAX8 as a potential oncology target.

The evidence goal is to find mechanisms that can control PAX protein folding, conformational state, stability, degradation, nuclear entry, nuclear retention, or nuclear accumulation. Include protein-protein interactions between PAX family members and partner transcription factors, cofactors, chromatin remodelers, ubiquitin/proteasome machinery, chaperones, kinases, SUMO/ubiquitin/other post-translational modification enzymes, import/export factors, and other direct protein-level regulators when the paper has mechanistic relevance to PAX protein abundance, localization, retention, or stability.

Exclude or deprioritize papers focused only on transcriptional regulation of PAX gene expression, genomic binding sites, downstream target genes, developmental gene-regulatory networks, lineage markers, or PAX-driven transcriptional programs unless they also contain direct evidence about PAX protein stability, folding, degradation, nuclear localization/retention, or PAX protein-protein interactions with plausible effects on those properties.
