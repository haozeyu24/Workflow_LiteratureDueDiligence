# Request

I want literature support for a review article on protein-level regulation of
PAX-family transcription factors. The main scientific question is what
mechanisms control PAX protein folding, stability, degradation, nuclear entry,
nuclear retention, or nuclear accumulation.

The review should also include studies where protein-protein interactions
between a PAX transcription factor and a partner transcription factor, cofactor,
or chromatin remodeler affect PAX protein stability or nuclear retention.

I am not interested in transcriptional regulation of PAX expression or papers
focused only on downstream genes regulated by PAX factors. The practical reason
is to understand what mechanisms might be disrupted to destabilize PAX8 as an
oncogenic protein, but the literature search itself should not focus on PAX8
alone. Similar mechanisms from other PAX family members are first-class
evidence, not just fallback background.
