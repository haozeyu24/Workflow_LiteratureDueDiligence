# Topic

This run asks how PAX-family transcription factors are regulated after
translation in ways that affect protein abundance, conformational state,
partner binding, nuclear localization, nuclear retention, or degradation.

The search should treat family-wide mechanism discovery as the primary goal.
PAX8 matters because it is the downstream interpretation target for oncology,
but early retrieval should not suppress informative PAX2, PAX3, PAX5, or PAX7
mechanistic papers.

Relevant evidence includes:

- direct measurement of PAX protein stability, half-life, turnover, or
  steady-state abundance
- ubiquitination, deubiquitination, proteasomal degradation, autophagic or
  lysosomal handling
- post-translational modifications that alter stability or localization
- folding or chaperone dependence
- nuclear import, export, retention, or sequestration
- PPI with partner transcription factors, cofactors, chromatin regulators, or
  modifying enzymes that changes PAX protein behavior

The biological nuance for reviewers is that a paper can be highly valuable even
if it is not about PAX8, as long as it provides a concrete and transferable
mechanism of PAX protein-level regulation. Conversely, a PAX8 paper can still
be low value if it addresses only expression, marker status, or downstream
transcription.

Pass-2 emphasis:

- retain strong family-wide mechanism papers as first-class evidence
- enrich direct `PAX8` PTM, localization, and stability papers when present
- reduce false positives from `Pax8-Cre`, `Pax7-CreER`, diagnostic-marker use,
  and papers where `Pax` appears incidentally rather than as the regulated
  protein

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers:
  PAX-family names plus protein stability, degradation, PTM, localization,
  chaperone, and interaction terms
- Required abstract claim shape:
  a PAX-family protein plus a plausible protein-level mechanism plus an outcome
  affecting abundance, degradation, localization, or retention
- Comparator scope:
  all PAX family members are in-scope for first-pass retrieval
- Evidence insufficient by itself:
  expression markers, transcriptional control, target-gene regulation, and
  recombinase-driver usage
- Secondary synthesis context:
  how family-wide mechanisms may create actionable vulnerabilities for PAX8
- Deferred adjacent biology:
  non-mechanistic developmental and lineage-identity literature
