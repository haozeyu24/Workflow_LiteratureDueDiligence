# Instruction

Identify literature that can explain how PAX8 protein abundance, folding, stability, nuclear localization/retention/accumulation, or degradation are controlled, and how those control points might reveal theoretically disruptable mechanisms for targeting oncogenic PAX8.

Prioritize mechanistic studies over expression-only, diagnostic-marker-only, or purely prognostic papers. Direct PAX8 evidence is highest priority. PAX-family comparator evidence is in scope when it concerns protein folding/chaperone dependence, post-translational stability, ubiquitin/proteasome or lysosome degradation, nuclear localization/import/export/retention, nuclear accumulation, or regulation of paired-box/homeodomain transcription factor protein turnover.

Do not broaden the primary retrieval scope to generic thyroid, ovarian, kidney, Mullerian, lineage, or transcriptional-network biology unless the abstract or full text connects that biology to PAX8 protein stability, folding, nuclear localization/retention/accumulation, or degradation.

Desired evidence classes:

- Direct biochemical or cell-biological mechanisms controlling PAX8 protein folding, stability, localization, retention, accumulation, or degradation.
- PAX8 post-translational modifications or interaction partners that alter protein abundance, half-life, localization, degradation, or oncogenic dependency.
- PAX8-relevant ubiquitin ligases, deubiquitinases, chaperones, proteasome/lysosome/autophagy mechanisms, nuclear import/export factors, or nuclear retention mechanisms.
- Comparator mechanisms in PAX family members, especially PAX2, PAX3, PAX5, PAX6, and PAX7, when likely transferable to PAX8 regulation.

Exclude papers that only use PAX8 as a diagnostic marker, lineage marker, antibody control, developmental expression marker, or clinical classifier unless they include mechanistic evidence about the requested protein-level regulation.

## Query Scope Contract

- primary entities: PAX8, paired box 8, paired-box gene 8, PAX-8.
- declared mechanism classes for PubMed retrieval: protein folding/chaperone dependence; protein stability/abundance/half-life; degradation/turnover/ubiquitin/proteasome/lysosome/autophagy; nuclear localization/import/export/retention/accumulation.
- authorized comparator entities or systems: other PAX transcription factor family members when paired with the declared mechanism classes.
- secondary context for synthesis only: cancer setting, oncogene dependency, thyroid/ovarian/renal/Mullerian lineage biology, transcriptional targets, chromatin, developmental biology.
- adjacent biology deferred from first-pass retrieval: broad PAX8 expression, diagnosis, prognosis, lineage identity, and pathway papers without protein-level regulation terms.
