# Topic

## Primary Entity

- PAX8 / paired box 8 / paired-box gene 8 / PAX-8.

## Primary Mechanism Classes

- Protein folding and chaperone dependence.
- Protein stability, abundance, half-life, stabilization, destabilization.
- Degradation, turnover, ubiquitination, proteasome, lysosome, autophagy, E3 ligases, deubiquitinases.
- Nuclear localization, nuclear import, nuclear export, nuclear retention, nuclear accumulation, subcellular localization.

## Comparator Scope

- Other PAX transcription factor family members are eligible as comparator evidence when the paper studies one of the primary mechanism classes. Include PAX2, PAX3, PAX5, PAX6, PAX7, PAX9, and paired-box transcription factors.

## Secondary Context

- Cancer settings, oncogene addiction, thyroid cancer, ovarian cancer, renal cancer, Mullerian lineage identity, developmental biology, transcriptional targets, chromatin regulation, and lineage survival are secondary context. They should support interpretation but should not drive first-pass retrieval unless paired with a primary mechanism class.

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers: PAX8 plus one primary mechanism term family; PAX family comparators plus one primary mechanism term family.
- Comparator scope: PAX family mechanisms that plausibly inform PAX8 protein-level regulation.
- Secondary synthesis context: oncogenic PAX8 dependency and cancer lineage biology.
- Deferred adjacent biology: broad expression, pathology-marker, developmental-pattern, or transcriptional target studies without folding, stability, localization, retention, accumulation, or degradation terms.
