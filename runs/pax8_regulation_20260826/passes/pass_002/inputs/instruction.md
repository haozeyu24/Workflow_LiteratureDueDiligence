# Instruction

Identify literature that can explain how PAX8 protein abundance, folding, stability, nuclear localization/retention/accumulation, or degradation are controlled, and how those control points might reveal theoretically disruptable mechanisms for targeting oncogenic PAX8.

Pass 2 incorporates PMC learning from `pmc_learning_1`: direct PAX8 PMC-readable evidence was sparse, while high-value comparator mechanisms repeatedly involved SUMOylation/deSUMOylation, phosphorylation-dependent stabilization, acetylation-dependent ubiquitination, monoubiquitination, proteasomal degradation, Hsp90 client dependence, targeted degradation/PROTAC, and nuclear localization/domain defects.

Prioritize:

- Direct PAX8 protein stability, SUMOylation, ubiquitination, degradation, half-life, nuclear localization, functional-domain, or nuclear localization signal studies.
- PAX8 post-translational modifications or interaction partners that alter protein abundance, half-life, localization, degradation, or oncogenic dependency.
- Comparator PAX-family studies where the PAX protein itself is modified, stabilized, destabilized, localized, retained, ubiquitinated, sumoylated, degraded, chaperone-dependent, or directly targeted for degradation.
- Mechanistic PAX3/PAX3-FOXO1, PAX7, PAX5, PAX6, and PAX2 papers when they reveal transferable PAX-family protein control logic.

Exclude:

- PAX8-AS1 lncRNA papers unless they separately study PAX8 protein regulation.
- Papers where PAX8 or another PAX protein regulates a downstream target protein but the PAX protein itself is not mechanistically regulated.
- Expression-only, diagnostic-marker-only, prognostic, lineage-marker, broad developmental, or clinical-classifier papers.
- Generic proteolysis, autophagy, muscle atrophy, or disease mechanism papers where PAX is incidental.

## Query Scope Contract

- primary entities: PAX8, paired box 8, paired-box gene 8, PAX-8.
- declared mechanism classes for PubMed retrieval: protein folding/chaperone dependence; protein stability/abundance/protein levels/half-life; degradation/turnover/ubiquitin/proteasome/lysosome/autophagy; SUMOylation/deSUMOylation; phosphorylation/acetylation/hydroxylation when tied to PAX protein level or localization; nuclear localization/import/export/retention/accumulation and functional domains.
- authorized comparator entities or systems: other PAX transcription factor family members when paired with the declared mechanism classes and when the PAX protein itself is regulated.
- secondary context for synthesis only: cancer setting, oncogene dependency, thyroid/ovarian/renal/Mullerian lineage biology, transcriptional targets, chromatin, developmental biology.
- adjacent biology deferred from retrieval: broad PAX8 expression, diagnosis, prognosis, lineage identity, target-gene expression, and pathway papers without PAX protein-level regulation terms.
