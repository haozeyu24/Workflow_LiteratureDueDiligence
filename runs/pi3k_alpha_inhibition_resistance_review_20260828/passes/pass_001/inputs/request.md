# Request

Identify and screen literature to support a review article on mechanisms of resistance to PI3K-alpha inhibition in cancer treatment. Include mechanisms observed in laboratory models and mechanisms inferred from clinical samples, acquired resistance, intrinsic resistance, trial outcomes, progression, and combination strategies designed to overcome PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance.

The review scope should span approved and investigational PI3K-alpha-selective or PI3K-alpha-mutant/selective inhibitors, including alpelisib, inavolisib, STX478, RLY-2608/RLy2608, and other relevant agents. The clinical-trial evidence goal includes published clinical trial papers across phases, positive and negative studies, and trial designs testing combinations that may address resistance.
