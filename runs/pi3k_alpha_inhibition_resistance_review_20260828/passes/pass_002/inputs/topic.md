# Topic

Mechanisms of resistance to PI3K-alpha inhibition in cancer treatment, spanning laboratory models, patient-derived translational evidence, clinical trial biomarker analyses, and clinical trial outcomes for approved and investigational PI3K-alpha-directed inhibitors.

The clinical scope includes breast cancer and other PIK3CA-mutant or PI3K-alpha-dependent solid tumors where PI3K-alpha inhibitors have been tested. Agents of interest include alpelisib, inavolisib, STX478, RLY-2608/RLy2608, and other PI3K-alpha-selective, PI3K-alpha-sparing, mutant-selective, or PIK3CA-directed investigational inhibitors identified by the search.

Resistance evidence may include acquired resistance after PI3K-alpha inhibitor exposure, baseline biomarkers of nonresponse, bypass signaling, pathway reactivation, secondary genomic alterations, transcriptomic or proteomic adaptation, tumor microenvironment-mediated resistance, and combination regimens designed to overcome or prevent resistance.

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers: PI3K-alpha/PIK3CA inhibitor drug names; PI3K-alpha/p110-alpha terminology; resistance, progression, nonresponse, biomarker, and clinical-trial terms; named bypass and adaptive mechanism classes from the instruction.
- Comparator scope: comparator drugs or pathways may be queried only when directly connected to PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance, a PI3K-alpha clinical regimen, or a trial combination strategy.
- Secondary synthesis context: general PIK3CA oncogenic biology, PI3K pathway signaling, adverse events, dose intensity, and subtype context can be used to interpret included papers.
- Deferred adjacent biology: broad resistance to endocrine therapy, pan-PI3K inhibitors, AKT inhibitors, mTOR inhibitors, or unrelated targeted therapies should not be primary retrieval scope unless tied to PI3K-alpha inhibition.
