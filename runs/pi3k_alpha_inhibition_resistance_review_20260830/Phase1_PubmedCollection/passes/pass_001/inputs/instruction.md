# Instruction

Support a review of resistance mechanisms to PI3K-alpha inhibition in cancer treatment. The run should identify laboratory/mechanistic studies and patient-facing clinical evidence that explain intrinsic resistance, acquired resistance, adaptive bypass, progression after response, incomplete response, or combination strategies intended to overcome resistance to PI3K-alpha inhibitors.

Prioritize papers that directly connect PI3K-alpha, PIK3CA, p110-alpha, or PI3K-alpha-selective inhibitors to resistance, sensitivity loss, pathway reactivation, bypass signaling, lineage or genotype-specific non-response, clinical progression, trial outcomes, or rational combinations. Include both approved and investigational PI3K-alpha inhibitors, including alpelisib, inavolisib, STX-478/STX478, RLY-2608/RLY2608, and clear class synonyms.

Prioritize clinical trial reports when they evaluate PI3K-alpha inhibitors, report response or progression outcomes, include resistance-relevant biomarker or subgroup analyses, or test combinations motivated by resistance biology. Preserve papers from any clinical phase, including positive, negative, failed, terminated, or combination trials, when the abstract suggests they may inform why treatment succeeds, fails, or is combined with another therapy.

Deprioritize papers that discuss generic PI3K pathway biology without PI3K-alpha, PIK3CA, p110-alpha, or a PI3K-alpha inhibitor anchor. Deprioritize papers about non-alpha PI3K isoforms unless they are explicit comparators for PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance. Deprioritize broad cancer reviews, drug-delivery-only papers, toxicity-only papers, pharmacokinetic-only papers, or trial-design-only records unless they contain resistance, response, progression, biomarker, or combination rationale useful for the review.

A useful final reading set should contain typed evidence across mechanism classes, drug examples, and clinical contexts: direct resistance mechanisms, adaptive/bypass pathways, biomarker/genotype associations, trial outcome evidence, and combination-strategy evidence. Full-text-unavailable but abstract-relevant clinical or mechanistic papers should remain visible as access cases rather than being treated as scientific exclusions.

This is a review-like downstream request. Use `review_frame.md` to preserve field framing, clinical context, and review-architecture needs without letting broad PI3K/cancer context become a standalone first-pass PubMed driver.
