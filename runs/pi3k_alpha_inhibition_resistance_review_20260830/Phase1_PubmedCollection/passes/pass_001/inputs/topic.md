# Topic

The run focuses on resistance to PI3K-alpha inhibition in cancer treatment across preclinical studies, translational patient evidence, and clinical trial literature.

Primary drug/entity vocabulary includes PI3K-alpha, PIK3CA, p110-alpha, PI3K-alpha selective inhibitors, alpelisib, inavolisib, STX-478/STX478, RLY-2608/RLY2608, and investigational or approved inhibitors clearly described as PI3K-alpha inhibitors.

Mechanism vocabulary should capture intrinsic and acquired resistance, adaptive feedback, pathway reactivation, bypass signaling, loss of sensitivity, treatment failure, progression, relapse, resistant models, biomarker-defined response or non-response, and combination strategies intended to overcome resistance.

Clinical vocabulary should capture clinical trial papers across phases, including phase 1, phase 2, phase 3, combination trials, negative or failed studies, subgroup reports, biomarker reports, progression-free survival, objective response, clinical benefit, progression, relapse, and safety or tolerability only when connected to dose, combinations, discontinuation, or therapeutic feasibility relevant to resistance.

Expected high-value evidence types include cell-line or organoid resistance models, xenograft or patient-derived model studies, patient biopsy or ctDNA analyses, biomarker subgroup analyses, trial outcome reports, and combination-rationale studies that explicitly pair PI3K-alpha inhibition with another intervention.

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers: PI3K-alpha/PIK3CA/p110-alpha or named PI3K-alpha inhibitors plus resistance/progression/response/failure/adaptive/bypass/combination/trial evidence.
- Required abstract claim shape: a PI3K-alpha entity or named inhibitor plus at least one declared resistance, response, progression, clinical-trial, biomarker, or combination claim.
- Comparator scope: non-alpha PI3K, AKT, mTOR, HER2, ER, MAPK, or other pathway terms may support inclusion only when the abstract ties them to PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance, clinical response, progression, or combination design.
- Evidence insufficient by itself: broad PIK3CA mutation prevalence, generic pathway diagrams, general cancer growth, broad PI3K inhibitor toxicity, or trial registry/design language without result, resistance, response, progression, biomarker, or combination rationale.
- Secondary synthesis context: breast cancer and other tumor-type context; endocrine resistance context; precision oncology; approval history; pathway background; class-wide toxicity and dose feasibility.
- Deferred adjacent biology: standalone AKT/mTOR/MAPK/ERBB/ESR1/PTEN/RTK/immune/metabolic/cell-cycle biology, unless paired to the declared PI3K-alpha inhibition resistance question.
