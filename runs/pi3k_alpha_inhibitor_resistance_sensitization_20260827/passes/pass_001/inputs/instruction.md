# Instruction

Identify papers that can help explain resistance to PI3K-alpha inhibition and strategies that sensitize cancers, tumor models, or molecular systems to PI3K-alpha inhibitors.

Prioritize papers that discuss PI3K-alpha / PIK3CA-directed inhibitors, including alpelisib, inavolisib, RLY-2608 / RLY2608, or closely related PI3K-alpha-selective agents, when paired with resistance, acquired resistance, adaptive response, sensitivity, sensitization, resensitization, rational combinations, or bypass signaling.

Carry forward mechanistic and translational studies that connect inhibitor response to specific pathways, mutations, biomarkers, drug combinations, signaling feedback, pathway rewiring, lineage/state changes, metabolic changes, or tumor microenvironment effects.

Deprioritize papers that mention PI3K signaling only as broad background, papers focused only on non-alpha PI3K isoforms without PI3K-alpha inhibitor relevance, purely pharmacokinetic/formulation papers without response mechanism, and broad oncology papers without inhibitor-resistance or sensitization evidence.

Useful final reading-set papers should support one or more of:

- a proposed or demonstrated resistance mechanism
- a sensitizing combination or perturbation
- evidence distinguishing orthosteric and allosteric PI3K-alpha inhibitor behavior
- a biomarker or molecular feature linked to resistance or sensitivity
- a model system or clinical cohort with response/resistance data for PI3K-alpha inhibition

## Query Scope Contract

- primary entities: PI3K-alpha, PIK3CA, p110-alpha, PI3K alpha-selective inhibitors, alpelisib/BYL719, inavolisib/GDC-0077, RLY-2608/RLY2608, and closely related PI3K-alpha inhibitors.
- declared mechanism classes for PubMed retrieval: inhibitor resistance, acquired resistance, adaptive feedback, bypass signaling, pathway rewiring, sensitization, resensitization, rational drug combinations, response biomarkers, resistance mutations, mutant-selective/allosteric inhibitor response.
- authorized comparator entities or systems: other PI3K-alpha-selective inhibitors and pan-PI3K or PI3K/mTOR inhibitors only when the abstract explicitly links the result back to PI3K-alpha, PIK3CA mutation, alpelisib, inavolisib, RLY-2608, or PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance/sensitivity.
- secondary context for synthesis only: endocrine therapy resistance, HER2/ERBB signaling, mTOR/AKT/MAPK pathway biology, tumor lineage/state effects, and metabolic adaptation when not directly tied to PI3K-alpha inhibitor response.
- adjacent biology deferred from first-pass retrieval: generic PI3K pathway signaling, broad breast cancer treatment, general targeted-therapy resistance, and immunotherapy response unless paired with a PI3K-alpha inhibitor or PIK3CA-directed response mechanism.
