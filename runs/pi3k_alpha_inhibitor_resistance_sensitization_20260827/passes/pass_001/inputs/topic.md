# Topic

PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance and sensitization.

The run should search for mechanistic and translational literature about why systems become resistant or remain insensitive to PI3K-alpha inhibitors, and what perturbations or combinations can improve response.

Drug examples include alpelisib / BYL719, inavolisib / GDC-0077, RLY-2608 / RLY2608, and other PI3K-alpha-selective or PIK3CA-mutant-selective inhibitors when the resistance or sensitization evidence is relevant.

## Retrieval Scope Notes

- PubMed query drivers: PI3K-alpha/PIK3CA/p110-alpha inhibitor terms paired with resistance, adaptive response, bypass pathway, sensitization, resensitization, combination therapy, biomarker, and mutant-selective/allosteric inhibitor terms.
- Comparator scope: pan-PI3K, PI3K/mTOR, AKT, mTOR, MAPK, ERBB/HER2, estrogen receptor/endocrine therapy, CDK4/6, SHP2, MEK, and related combination contexts only when tied to PI3K-alpha inhibitor response or PIK3CA mutation.
- Secondary synthesis context: breast cancer, endocrine-resistant breast cancer, PIK3CA-mutant solid tumors, clinical trial resistance biomarkers, feedback activation, pathway rebound, lineage or phenotypic state, and metabolic adaptation.
- Deferred adjacent biology: generic PI3K pathway biology, general targeted-therapy resistance, or broad oncology treatment without PI3K-alpha inhibitor response evidence.
